Hi, my name is Michael.

Software engineer, artist, and machine learning enthusiast in San Francisco.

I'm part of a rare bunch of people who have gone to art school for Animation and then went back to school for Applied Mathematics.

I come from the small, rural town of South Bend, WA. I made it out and traveled to San Francisco to attend school and live in an art collective. Soon after I started to code, out of an interest in the intersection of digital synthesizers and machine learning models, and that lead me to eventually decided to get a degree in Mathematics.

I've written a lot of music. It has been one of my greatest teachers. It's taught me how one develops taste by trying new ideas quickly. It's how I approach almost everything in life. Start something, anything, and throw as many ideas out as you can. Only keep what sticks, and trust your gut when you know it isn't right.

Lately (in my freetime) I've been experimenting making different AI harnesess, thinking about Evals and new data sets, exploring new modalities of human/computer interaction, and of course making art.
