Hi, my name is Michael.

Software engineer, artist, and machine learning enthusiast in San Francisco.

I come from the small, rural town of South Bend, WA. I moved to the city in 2010 at 18 to live and work in an art collective. At 26 I started to code, out of an interest in the intersection of digital synthesizers and machine learning models, and at 28 went to college for a degree in Mathematics.

After graduating I dove into contract work and found I loved the variety. My software has helped manage large-scale tours for artists like The Weeknd and improve cancer diagnostics at UCSF. Today I’m building Headliner, software for live music finance.

In my free time I investigate different modalities of human-computer interaction: novel audio synthesizers, fine-tuning ML models, and whatever extends what people can do.
